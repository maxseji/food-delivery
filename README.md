## Food Delivery Service

This project aims to aid people in regards to smooth delivery services as most struggle with maneuvering most delivery apps. It solves complicated ordering processes.

## Best Delivery Practices:
- Food safety and packaging: Keep hot/cold temps safe, use leak-proof/tamper-evident packaging, double-check orders, and avoid fragile menu items.
- Delivery and rider ops: Sync prep with pickup, plan smart routes, and prioritize rider safety and peak-hour coverage.
- Customer and business health: Share clear ETAs, fix issues fast, and price fees/menus to protect your margins.
